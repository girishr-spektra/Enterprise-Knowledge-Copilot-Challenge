## Purpose

This policy governs all purchasing and procurement activity at Contoso Corporation. It establishes the approval thresholds, vendor selection requirements, purchasing channels, and compliance obligations that apply when committing company funds to goods or services.

All employees initiating purchases on behalf of Contoso are required to comply with this policy regardless of the funding source (operating budget, project budget, capital expenditure, or purchase card).

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- All direct spend on goods and services by Contoso employees
- Software licenses, SaaS subscriptions, and cloud resource commitments
- Professional services engagements including consulting, legal, audit, and staffing
- Capital expenditure including hardware, equipment, and facility improvements
- Renewals of existing contracts

It does not apply to payroll, employee benefits (governed by HR), or intercompany transactions.

## Procurement Channels

| Purchase Type | Required Channel | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard goods and services | Approved suppliers in the Procurement portal | Search approved vendor list first |
| Software and SaaS subscriptions | IT-led procurement with IT security review | Submit via IT portal; triggers IT-003 vendor assessment |
| Professional services (consulting, legal) | Competitive tender or pre-approved panel | Panel list in Procurement portal |
| Emergency or urgent purchase | Emergency purchase process (Section 8) | Requires CFO pre-approval |
| Purchase Card (P-Card) | For purchases under $500 from non-catalog vendors | Card issuance requires manager approval |

## Approval Thresholds

All purchases require approval at the appropriate level before a purchase order is issued or a commitment is made. Verbal or informal approval is not sufficient.

| Purchase Value | Approval Level Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Under $500 | Manager approval | P-Card permitted; receipt required |
| $500 - $4,999 | Manager + Department Director | Via Procurement portal |
| $5,000 - $24,999 | Director + VP of Function | Formal purchase order required |
| $25,000 - $99,999 | VP of Function + CFO | Competitive quotations required (minimum 3) |
| $100,000 - $499,999 | CFO + CEO | Full RFP process required; Legal review |
| $500,000 and above | CFO + CEO + Board approval | Board resolution required |

Budget ownership does not override the approval threshold. A department VP spending within their approved budget must still follow the threshold approval requirements.

## Vendor Selection Requirements

### Minimum Competitive Quotation Requirements

| Purchase Value | Minimum Quotations | Exceptions |
|---|---|---|
| Under $5,000 | 1 (single source acceptable) | None |
| $5,000 - $24,999 | 2 | Sole source justification accepted with Director approval |
| $25,000 - $99,999 | 3 | Sole source justification accepted with CFO approval |
| $100,000 and above | Full RFP required | Sole source requires Board approval |

A Sole Source Justification form must be submitted when competitive quotations are waived. Acceptable sole source justifications include: unique technical capability, existing enterprise contract, emergency situation, or intellectual property compatibility.

### Vendor Evaluation Criteria

When evaluating suppliers, the following criteria must be considered and documented:

| Criterion | Weight Guidance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Price and total cost of ownership | 25-40% | Include implementation, training, support costs |
| Technical capability and fit | 20-30% | Alignment with requirements specification |
| Vendor financial stability | 10-15% | Public financials or credit check for contracts over $50,000 |
| Security and compliance posture | 15-20% | IT-003 assessment result for technology vendors |
| References and track record | 10-15% | Minimum 2 reference calls for contracts over $25,000 |
| Contractual terms and risk | 5-10% | Payment terms, liability caps, termination rights |

Evaluation scores must be documented in the Procurement portal for all purchases over $25,000.

## Preferred and Approved Suppliers

Contoso maintains a list of preferred and approved suppliers in the Procurement portal. Preferred suppliers have negotiated terms, pre-agreed security assessments, and streamlined contracting.

| Supplier Category | Use Preferred Supplier First | Justification Required to Use Alternative |
|---|---|---|

| Travel (hotels and rental cars) | Yes (Concur preferred list) | Yes - availability or location |

## Purchase Order Process

All purchases over $500 must be accompanied by a Purchase Order (PO) issued through the Procurement portal before goods or services are received.

| Step | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Requester creates purchase request with specification and budget code | Requester |
| 2 | Manager and approval chain approves per threshold table | Approvers |
| 3 | Procurement reviews and issues PO to vendor | Procurement team |
| 4 | Goods or services delivered; requester confirms receipt in portal | Requester |
| 5 | Finance matches invoice to PO and confirms delivery | Finance |
| 6 | Payment processed per agreed payment terms | Finance / Accounts Payable |

Three-way match (PO, receipt, invoice) is required for all invoices over $1,000. Invoices without a PO reference will be returned to vendor unpaid.

## Contract Requirements

| Contract Value | Review Required | Standard Terms Available |
|---|---|---|
| Under $5,000 | Manager and Procurement only | Yes - use standard Contoso vendor agreement |
| $5,000 - $49,999 | Procurement review | Yes - may use Contoso standard or vendor terms with redlines |
| $50,000 - $249,999 | Legal review required | Legal negotiates if vendor terms used |
| $250,000 and above | Legal + external counsel review | Full negotiation; Legal lead |

All contracts must include: scope of services, pricing, payment terms, intellectual property ownership, confidentiality, data protection (if applicable), termination rights, and liability limits. Contoso's standard payment terms are Net 30.

---

## Recurring and Subscription Spend

Software subscriptions and recurring service contracts require:

- Annual review by the business owner to confirm continued business need
- IT confirmation that the software is still in use (for software subscriptions over $1,000/year)
- Renewal approval at the same threshold as the original purchase value
- Notification to Procurement at least 60 days before auto-renewal for contracts over $10,000

Failure to notify Procurement before auto-renewal does not release the company from the renewed obligation, but the responsible employee may be subject to a formal review.

---

## Emergency Procurement

When a purchase must be made urgently outside the standard process:

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| CFO verbal pre-approval | Required before commitment |
| Formal approval submission | Within 24 hours of purchase |
| Competitive process | Waived; single source acceptable |
| Post-purchase PO | Required within 1 business day |
| Emergency justification on file | Required for audit purposes |

Abuse of the emergency procurement process is a disciplinary matter.

---

## Non-Compliant Purchases

Purchases made outside this policy (unauthorized commitments) are the personal financial responsibility of the employee who made them if the company elects not to ratify the purchase. Unauthorized commitments above $5,000 are subject to formal disciplinary action.

Finance will not process payment for non-compliant purchases until a retroactive approval is obtained from the CFO.
