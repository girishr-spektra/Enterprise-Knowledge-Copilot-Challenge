## Purpose

This document defines Contoso Corporation's annual budget process, mid-cycle reforecast procedures, and the approval requirements for budget requests, reallocation, and unbudgeted spend. It ensures financial planning is rigorous, aligned to strategic priorities, and executed with appropriate governance.

---

## Scope

This process applies to all Contoso business units, departments, and cost centers. It covers operating expenditure (OpEx), capital expenditure (CapEx), headcount, and project budgets.

---

## Budget Cycle Overview

| Phase | Timing | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic planning alignment | July - August | Executive team sets priorities and envelope targets for the following year |
| Bottom-up budget submission | September - October | Business units submit detailed budget requests via the FP&A portal |
| Finance review and challenge | October - November | FP&A reviews all submissions; challenge sessions with budget owners |
| Executive approval | November | CFO and CEO review consolidated budget; board pre-read |
| Board approval | December | Board approves full-year budget for next fiscal year |
| Budget loaded into systems | January 1 | Approved budget loaded into ERP for actuals tracking |
| Q1 reforecast | April | First reforecast; material variance review |
| Q2 reforecast | July | Mid-year reforecast; headcount and project adjustments |
| Q3 reforecast | October | Third reforecast; year-end projection finalized |

The fiscal year runs January 1 through December 31.

---

## Budget Submission Requirements

All budget submissions must include:

| Element | Description | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Prior year actuals | Actual spend from the preceding fiscal year by category | Yes |
| Current year forecast | Latest estimate of current year spend | Yes |
| Year-on-year change explanation | Narrative justification for increases over 10% or absolute increases over $50,000 | Yes |
| Headcount plan | Requested headcount by role, start date, and full-year cost | Yes |
| Capital expenditure requests | Itemized CapEx with business case for items over $25,000 | Yes for CapEx |
| Strategic initiative costs | Costs tied to named strategic initiatives, cross-referenced to strategy map | Yes if applicable |
| Cost reduction initiatives | Planned savings with method, timing, and owner | Yes if savings planned |
| Assumptions log | Key assumptions underlying the submission (revenue, pricing, headcount ramp) | Yes |

Incomplete submissions are returned by FP&A within 3 business days of review.

## Budget Approval Authority

### Operating Expenditure (OpEx)

| Budget Level | Approval Authority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Department OpEx under $500,000 per year | VP of Function | Within approved budget envelope |
| Department OpEx $500,000 - $2M per year | VP of Function + CFO | Annual budget cycle |
| Department OpEx over $2M per year | VP + CFO + CEO | Annual budget cycle |
| Total company OpEx | Board of Directors | Annual approval |

### Capital Expenditure (CapEx)

| CapEx Amount | Approval Authority | Process |
|---|---|---|
| Under $25,000 | VP of Function | Within approved CapEx envelope |
| $25,000 - $99,999 | VP + CFO | CapEx request form with business case |
| $100,000 - $499,999 | VP + CFO + CEO | Full business case; NPV or ROI required |
| $500,000 and above | CFO + CEO + Board | Board resolution required |

### Headcount

| Request Type | Approval Authority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Backfill of existing approved role | VP of Function + HR | No net headcount increase |
| New role within approved headcount plan | VP + HR | Annual budget cycle |
| New role outside approved headcount plan | VP + CFO + HR | Unbudgeted headcount form required |
| Executive hire (Director and above) | CEO + CPO | Regardless of budget status |

## Mid-Year Budget Adjustments

### Reforecast Process

The Q1, Q2, and Q3 reforecasts allow departments to update projections. Reforecasts do not reset the approved budget baseline - they provide a best estimate of year-end outturn.

| Reforecast Submission | Deadline | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 reforecast | April 15 | CFO approval of consolidated reforecast |
| Q2 reforecast | July 15 | CFO + CEO approval of mid-year reforecast |
| Q3 reforecast | October 15 | CFO approval of year-end projection |

### Budget Reallocation

Reallocation moves budget between line items or cost centers within the same approved total. Approval requirements:

| Reallocation Amount | Approval Required | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Under $10,000 | Finance Business Partner sign-off | Same day |
| $10,000 - $49,999 | VP of Function | Within 2 business days |
| $50,000 - $199,999 | VP + CFO | Within 3 business days |
| $200,000 and above | VP + CFO + CEO | Within 5 business days |

Reallocation between CapEx and OpEx categories requires CFO approval regardless of amount, as it affects asset capitalization and depreciation schedules.

## Unbudgeted Spend Requests

Spend not included in the approved budget requires a formal Unbudgeted Spend Request (USR) before commitment.

| Unbudgeted Amount | Approval Authority | Justification Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Under $5,000 | Manager + Finance Business Partner | Brief narrative |
| $5,000 - $24,999 | VP + Finance Business Partner | Business case summary |
| $25,000 - $99,999 | VP + CFO | Full business case with ROI or strategic alignment |
| $100,000 - $499,999 | VP + CFO + CEO | Full business case; quarterly Board disclosure |
| $500,000 and above | VP + CFO + CEO + Board | Board approval required |

USRs are processed through the FP&A portal. Average processing time: 3-5 business days for amounts under $25,000; 7-10 business days for amounts requiring CFO or CEO approval.

## Budget Monitoring and Variance Reporting

| Activity | Frequency | Owner | Distribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly actuals vs budget variance report | Monthly, by 10th of following month | Finance | VP and above |
| Department budget review meeting | Monthly | Finance Business Partner with department VP | Department leadership |
| Material variance escalation | When variance exceeds 10% unfavorable or $50,000 | Finance | CFO, department VP |
| Year-to-date reforecast presentation | Quarterly | FP&A | CFO, CEO |
| Full company budget vs actuals | Monthly | Finance | CFO, CEO, Board |

A favorable variance (underspend) does not automatically create permission to spend the surplus elsewhere. Reallocation approval is required.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Budget Owner (VP or Director) | Submitting accurate budget requests; monitoring actuals; approving in-budget spend within their authority level; requesting USRs before committing unbudgeted funds |
| Finance Business Partner | Supporting budget preparation; challenging submissions; monitoring variances; advising on reallocation requests |
| FP&A team | Consolidating company budget; running the annual cycle and reforecast calendar; loading approved budgets into ERP |
| CFO | Approving budgets per authority matrix; presenting to Board; accountability for financial performance |
| Board of Directors | Approving total company budget; reviewing material variances |
