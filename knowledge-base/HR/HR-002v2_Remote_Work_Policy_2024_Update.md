# Remote Work Policy - 2024 Update

**Document ID:** HR-002v2  
**Category:** Human Resources - Work Arrangements  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** September 1, 2024  
**Review Date:** September 1, 2025  
**Document Owner:** VP, People Operations  
**Approved By:** Chief People Officer  
**Status:** Active - Supersedes HR-002 v2.1

---

## Purpose

This policy updates and replaces HR-002 v2.1 effective September 1, 2024. It reflects changes to remote work eligibility, allowance structures, equipment entitlements, and security requirements following the global workplace modernization initiative.

Key changes from HR-002 v2.1 are highlighted throughout this document.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all Contoso full-time and part-time employees in eligible roles with a formal remote or hybrid work arrangement. Contract staff arrangements continue to be governed by individual contracts.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

**UPDATED from v2.1:** Tenure requirement reduced. Performance standing requirement expanded. New "collaboration score" criterion added.

| Criterion | Requirement | Change from v2.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Role classification | Role must be designated as remote-eligible in HR systems | No change |
| Tenure | Minimum 3 months of continuous employment | Reduced from 6 months |
| Performance standing | Must not be on a formal PIP and must have a minimum "Meets Expectations" rating on last review | Expanded - rating threshold added |
| Manager approval | Written approval from direct manager required | No change |
| IT security compliance | Must complete Remote Security Awareness AND Data Handling Essentials modules | Added Data Handling requirement |
| Internet connectivity | Stable home connection of at least 50 Mbps download | Increased from 25 Mbps |
| Collaboration health | Team collaboration score (from Viva Insights) must not be flagged as "At Risk" | New criterion |

---

## Arrangement Types

**UPDATED from v2.1:** Occasional remote days per month increased. New "Globally Distributed" type added for international hires.

| Type | Definition | Maximum Remote Days | Change from v2.1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fully Remote | Primary work location is not a Contoso office | 5 days per week | No change |
| Hybrid - Flexible | Minimum 2 days per week in office, flexible scheduling | 3 days per week | No change |
| Hybrid - Fixed | Office attendance on specific designated days | As defined in agreement | No change |
| Occasional Remote | Ad-hoc remote work without a formal arrangement | Maximum 6 days per month | Increased from 4 days |
| Globally Distributed | Employee hired into a role with no local Contoso office | 5 days per week | New in v3.0 |

## Availability and Responsiveness

**UPDATED from v2.1:** Camera-on policy updated. New asynchronous communication standard introduced.

| Standard | Requirement | Change from v2.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Core hours online | 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time, responsive within 15 min | No change |
| Camera-on policy | Applies to all team meetings of 6 or fewer participants | Threshold increased from 4 |
| Response to messages | Within 2 hours during core hours via primary channel (Teams) | Codified; previously informal |
| Asynchronous documentation | For decisions made outside meetings, a written summary must be shared to the relevant Teams channel within 24 hours | New in v3.0 |
| Status accuracy | Teams and Outlook status must accurately reflect availability | No change |

---

## Data Security Requirements

**UPDATED from v2.1:** Public location restriction updated. Endpoint detection requirement added.

1. Company data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage accounts
2. VPN must be active when accessing any Contoso internal system - no exceptions
3. Physical documents with confidential content must be stored in a lockable space when not in use
4. Household members must not be present during video calls where confidential information is discussed
5. **Working in public locations is permitted only with both VPN active and a privacy screen filter attached** - previously prohibited entirely
6. **Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) software must be installed and active on all company devices** - new in v3.0
7. Employees must not use public Wi-Fi networks without an active VPN connection

---

## Approval Process

**UPDATED from v2.1:** Process streamlined. HR validation moved to parallel with IT.

| Step | Action | Owner | Timeline | Change from v2.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee submits remote work request via HR portal | Employee | Any time | No change |
| 2 | Manager reviews and approves or declines | Manager | Within 3 business days | Reduced from 5 days |
| 3 | HR validates eligibility and IT begins configuration in parallel | HR + IT | Within 3 business days of manager approval | Parallel process, previously sequential |
| 4 | Equipment ordered (if applicable) | IT | Within 5 business days of HR confirmation | Reduced from 7 days |
| 5 | Arrangement documented and communicated | HR | Before start date | No change |

---

## Arrangement Review and Termination

Annual reviews retained. Contoso may modify or terminate a remote work arrangement with 30 days' notice under the same conditions as v2.1, with the addition of:

- Persistent failure to meet asynchronous documentation standards
- Collaboration score sustained at "At Risk" level for 60 or more consecutive days
