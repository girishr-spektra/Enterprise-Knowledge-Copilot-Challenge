## Purpose

This Code of Conduct defines the standards of professional behavior expected of all employees, contractors, and third-party representatives acting on behalf of Contoso Corporation. It establishes the ethical and behavioral baseline that underpins a respectful, inclusive, and high-performance workplace.

All employees are required to read, understand, and comply with this document as a condition of continued employment. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- All full-time and part-time employees globally
- Temporary employees and agency workers
- Contractors and consultants with access to Contoso systems or premises
- Board members and executive officers

It applies in all work settings including Contoso offices, remote work environments, client sites, and at any event representing Contoso including conferences, trade shows, and business travel.

---

## Core Principles

Contoso's Code of Conduct is grounded in five core principles:

| Principle | Definition | Examples of Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Integrity | Act honestly and transparently in all business dealings | Accurate expense reporting; disclosing conflicts of interest |
| Respect | Treat all individuals with dignity regardless of background | Inclusive language; listening actively; avoiding interruptions |
| Accountability | Take ownership of outcomes and decisions | Acknowledging mistakes; meeting commitments; raising concerns |
| Confidentiality | Protect sensitive information entrusted to you | Not sharing customer data; securing login credentials |
| Compliance | Follow all applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies | Adhering to data privacy laws; completing mandatory training |

## Professional Conduct Standards

### 4.1 Workplace Interactions

Employees are expected to:

- Communicate professionally and constructively at all times, whether in writing, verbally, or through digital channels
- Avoid language, gestures, or imagery that could be perceived as offensive, discriminatory, or harassing
- Resolve disagreements through respectful dialogue or escalation to a manager or HR, not through personal attacks or public criticism
- Treat all colleagues as equals regardless of seniority, department, function, or geographic location

### 4.2 Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest arises when personal interests or relationships could impair professional judgment. Employees must:

| Situation | Required Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| External employment at a competitor | Disclose in writing to HR | Before accepting |
| Personal financial interest in a vendor or supplier | Disclose to manager and Legal | Within 5 business days of awareness |
| Family member employed by a Contoso vendor | Disclose to HR | Within 5 business days |
| Gifts received from vendors or clients exceeding $50 in value | Declare using the Gift Register form | Within 10 business days |
| Board membership or advisory role at another company | Written approval from CPO required | Before accepting |

Failure to disclose a conflict of interest is considered a violation of this policy even if no actual harm results.

### 4.3 Social Media and External Communications

- Employees may not disclose Contoso confidential information on personal social media channels
- Opinions shared publicly that could be interpreted as representing Contoso must include a clear disclaimer that the views are personal
- Employees representing Contoso officially in media, analyst calls, or public forums must coordinate with the Communications team in advance
- Employees may not make disparaging public statements about Contoso, its leadership, clients, or partners

### 4.4 Attendance and Reliability

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Punctuality | Arrive at or before the scheduled start time for meetings and shifts |
| Absence notification | Notify your manager before the scheduled start time if unable to attend |
| Repeated unplanned absences | Three or more within a 60-day period triggers a formal attendance review |
| Extended leave | Must be requested through HR in accordance with the Leave Policy (HR-004) |

## Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disciplinary review:

1. Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind - verbal, physical, or digital
2. Discrimination based on any protected characteristic including race, gender, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, or national origin
3. Theft, fraud, or misappropriation of company or client assets
4. Unauthorized access to systems, data, or premises
5. Falsification of records, timesheets, expense reports, or credentials
6. Possession or use of controlled substances on company premises or during work hours
7. Retaliation against any employee who raises a concern in good faith
8. Violation of client confidentiality or data protection obligations
9. Misuse of Contoso name, brand, or intellectual property for personal gain
10. Physical violence or threats of violence in any work-related context

---

## Reporting Obligations and Non-Retaliation

Employees who become aware of a violation of this Code are obligated to report it. Reporting channels include:

| Channel | Best For | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Direct manager | Non-sensitive workplace conduct issues | Business hours |
| HR Business Partner | Sensitive HR matters, conflicts of interest | Business hours |
| Ethics Hotline (1-800-CON-ETHI) | Anonymous reporting of fraud, harassment, serious violations | 24/7 |
| Legal and Compliance team | Regulatory violations, data breaches, legal exposure | Business hours |

Contoso strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports a concern in good faith. Any retaliation - even if the underlying concern turns out to be unfounded - is itself a violation of this policy.

---

## Disciplinary Framework

| Severity Level | Examples | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 - Minor | Isolated attendance issue, informal communication lapse | Verbal counseling, coaching |
| Level 2 - Moderate | Repeated minor violations, conflict of interest non-disclosure | Written warning, formal improvement plan |
| Level 3 - Serious | Harassment, deliberate policy violation, minor financial misconduct | Final written warning, suspension |
| Level 4 - Gross Misconduct | Fraud, theft, violence, data breach, discrimination | Immediate suspension pending investigation; termination |

Disciplinary decisions are made by HR in consultation with the relevant business leader and Legal where appropriate. Employees have the right to respond to allegations before a decision is made.

---

## Acknowledgment

All employees are required to confirm acknowledgment of this Code of Conduct annually through the HR portal. New employees must complete this acknowledgment within their first 5 business days.

Acknowledgment is recorded and forms part of the employee's personnel file. Failure to complete acknowledgment is a policy violation.
