**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

This policy governs all business travel and expense reimbursement for Contoso Corporation employees. It defines what is reimbursable, at what limits, through which process, and the approval requirements for each category of spend.

Employees are expected to apply good judgment and spend company funds as though they were their own, prioritizing cost-effectiveness without compromising safety, productivity, or the quality of business representation.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all Contoso employees, contractors, and anyone traveling on behalf of Contoso whose expenses will be reimbursed by the company.

---

## Booking and Pre-Approval Requirements

| Travel Type | Advance Booking Requirement | Pre-Approval Required | Approval Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic travel (same country) | Book at least 14 days in advance | For total trip cost exceeding $500 | Direct manager |
| International travel (cross-border) | Book at least 21 days in advance | Always required | Manager + VP of department |
| Customer or partner visits | Book at least 7 days in advance | For total trip cost exceeding $1,000 | Direct manager |
| Conference or event attendance | As early as registration opens | Always required | Manager + HR |
| Emergency travel | As needed | Post-approval within 24 hours | Manager (retroactive) |

All non-emergency bookings must be made through the Contoso approved travel portal (Concur). Direct bookings from non-approved channels are not reimbursable unless the Concur platform was unavailable and documented.

---

## Air Travel

| Class | Policy | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Economy | Required for all flights under 6 hours | No exceptions |
| Premium Economy | Permitted for flights 6-10 hours | With VP-level approval |
| Business Class | Permitted for flights over 10 hours | With VP-level approval |
| First Class | Not reimbursable under any circumstances | Personal upgrade permitted at own cost |

Advance purchase fares must be used when available. Refundable fares are permitted only when the traveler has documented uncertainty about travel dates.

Lounge access through personal credit cards is not reimbursable. Lounge access purchased for approved international business class travel is reimbursable.

## Accommodation

| Accommodation Type | Maximum Nightly Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic - Major cities (NY, SF, LA, Chicago, Boston) | $250 USD | Excluding taxes and fees |
| Domestic - Other cities | $175 USD | Excluding taxes and fees |
| International - Tier 1 (London, Paris, Tokyo, Singapore, Sydney) | $350 USD | Excluding taxes and fees |
| International - Other cities | $250 USD | Excluding taxes and fees |
| Extended stay (7+ nights) | Negotiated rate required | Contact travel@contoso.com |

Employees must use hotels from the Contoso preferred hotel program when available. Alternatives require documented unavailability or manager approval for rates exceeding limits.

Airbnb and short-term rental platforms are permitted only in locations where no hotel options are available within the rate limit, and require pre-approval.

## Business Entertainment

Meals or events with clients, partners, or prospects require:

1. Pre-approval from manager for expenses over $150 per person
2. Documentation of business purpose and attendees (names and company affiliation)
3. Submission within 30 days of the event

| Entertainment Limit | Per Person | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Client / partner meal | $150 USD | Per person, excluding gratuity |
| Client reception or event | $200 USD | Per person, all-in |
| Internal team meal | $50 USD | Per person; requires manager approval |
| Gifts to clients or partners | $75 USD | Per recipient per year; see HR-001 Section 4.2 |

Alcohol is reimbursable as part of a business entertainment expense only when it is incidental to a business meal with clients or partners. Standalone bar tabs are not reimbursable.

## Expense Submission and Reimbursement

| Step | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Submit expense report | Via Concur with itemized receipts | Within 30 days of trip end |
| Receipt requirement | Required for all expenses over $25 | Attach scan or photo in Concur |
| Manager approval | First-level approval in Concur | Within 5 business days of submission |
| Finance review | For reports over $1,000 total | Within 3 business days of manager approval |
| Reimbursement | Via payroll or direct deposit | Within 2 pay cycles of final approval |

Expense reports submitted more than 90 days after the expense date will not be reimbursed without CFO exception. Late submissions require a written explanation and manager endorsement.

---

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are never reimbursable:

- Personal entertainment, sightseeing, or leisure activities
- Fines, parking tickets, or traffic violations
- Visa or passport renewal fees for personal travel overlapping a business trip
- Travel insurance for personal travel extensions
- Purchases for personal use including clothing, gifts for personal relationships
- Upgrades not covered by policy (first class, premium hotel rooms beyond limits)
- Airline fees for extra baggage beyond one checked bag on trips under 5 nights
- Expenses already reimbursed by a client or third party

---

## Policy Violations

Employees who submit false, inflated, or non-compliant expense claims are subject to:

- Repayment of the non-compliant amount
- Formal disciplinary action under HR-001
- For deliberate fraud: termination and potential legal action

Finance conducts periodic audits of expense reports. Employees selected for audit are required to provide original receipts within 10 business days.
