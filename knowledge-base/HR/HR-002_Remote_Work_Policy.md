## Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for remote work at Contoso Corporation. It defines eligibility, responsibilities, equipment entitlements, and operational expectations for employees working from a location other than an approved Contoso office.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all Contoso full-time and part-time employees in eligible roles who have a formal remote or hybrid work arrangement. It does not apply to occasional ad-hoc remote work days (covered under manager discretion) or temporary remote arrangements during medical leave (covered under HR-004).

---

## Eligibility Criteria

Not all roles are eligible for remote work. Eligibility is determined by:

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Role classification | Role must be designated as remote-eligible in the HR systems |
| Tenure | Minimum 6 months of continuous employment at Contoso |
| Performance standing | Must not be on a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) |
| Manager approval | Written approval from direct manager required |
| IT security compliance | Must have completed the Remote Security Awareness training module |
| Internet connectivity | Must maintain a stable home internet connection of at least 25 Mbps download |

Eligibility does not guarantee approval. Business unit needs and team coverage requirements will also be considered.

---

## Arrangement Types

| Type | Definition | Maximum Remote Days |
|---|---|---|
| Fully Remote | Employee primary work location is not a Contoso office | 5 days per week |
| Hybrid - Flexible | Employee works from office a minimum of 2 days per week, scheduling is flexible | 3 days per week |
| Hybrid - Fixed | Employee works from office on specific designated days | As defined in agreement |
| Occasional Remote | Ad-hoc remote work not covered by a formal arrangement | Maximum 4 days per month |

Arrangement type is documented in the employment record and is subject to change based on business needs with a minimum 30-day notice period.

## Equipment and Technology

### Company-Provided Equipment

Contoso provides the following equipment to employees with approved remote arrangements:

| Item | Fully Remote | Hybrid | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop | Yes | Yes | Standard issue via IT |
| External monitor (single) | Yes | No | Submit IT request within 30 days of approval |
| Keyboard and mouse | Yes | No | Standard set, no peripherals substitution |
| Headset | Yes | Yes | For video conferencing |
| Mobile device (if role requires) | As per role | As per role | Requires separate approval |

### Home Office Allowance

Employees in Fully Remote arrangements are entitled to a one-time home office setup allowance of **$400 USD** to cover ergonomic or connectivity improvements. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of arrangement approval and are subject to manager approval. Receipts required.

Hybrid employees are not eligible for the home office allowance under version 2.1 of this policy.

### Internet Subsidy

| Arrangement | Monthly Subsidy | Claim Process |
|---|---|---|
| Fully Remote | $30 USD per month | Submit via Expense system monthly with ISP invoice |
| Hybrid - Flexible | $15 USD per month | Submit via Expense system monthly with ISP invoice |
| Hybrid - Fixed | Not applicable | - |

## Availability and Responsiveness

Remote employees are expected to maintain the same availability standards as office-based colleagues during core hours.

| Core Hours | Timezone | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM | Employee's local time | Online and responsive within 15 minutes |
| Outside core hours | Employee's local time | Reasonable effort; not required to respond immediately |
| Meetings | As scheduled | Camera-on policy applies for team meetings of 4 or fewer participants |

Employees must keep calendar availability current in Outlook and set accurate out-of-office status when unavailable during core hours.

---

## Data Security Requirements

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a secure work environment:

1. Company data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage accounts
2. VPN must be active when accessing any Contoso internal system or application
3. Physical documents containing confidential information must not be left visible or unattended at the remote workspace
4. Household members must not be present on video calls where confidential information is discussed
5. Employees are prohibited from working in public locations (coffee shops, hotels, airports) without VPN and a privacy screen filter

## Approval Process

| Step | Action | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee submits remote work request via HR portal | Employee | Any time |
| 2 | Manager reviews and approves or declines | Manager | Within 5 business days |
| 3 | HR validates eligibility criteria | HR | Within 3 business days |
| 4 | IT configures equipment order (if applicable) | IT | Within 7 business days of HR confirmation |
| 5 | Arrangement formally documented and communicated | HR | Before start date |

---

## Policy Review and Termination of Arrangement

Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually. Contoso reserves the right to modify or terminate a remote work arrangement with 30 days' notice if:

- Business requirements change
- Performance or collaboration issues arise linked to the remote arrangement
- The employee is placed on a Performance Improvement Plan
- A role change makes the arrangement incompatible with new responsibilities
